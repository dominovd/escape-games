3. The workshop: open Workbench

5. Workbench: use Bird key on Brass bird → get Brass gear
6. Workbench: Toolbox → solve "Toolbox lock" (answer: ⭐ ⚙️ 🌙)
7. Workbench: Crank → get Crank
8. Go back to The workshop
9. The workshop: open Gear train
10. Gear train: use Brass gear on Empty spindle
11. Go back to The workshop
12. The workshop: open Display case
13. Display case: Pocket watch → get Pocket watch
14. Go back to The workshop
15. The workshop: open Grandfather clock
16. Grandfather clock: use Crank on Clock face → solve "Set the clock" (answer: 4 15)
17. Go back to The workshop
18. The workshop: open Door
